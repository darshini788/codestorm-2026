## 🌟 Key Features of CampusConnect
1. **Role-Based Access Control:** Secure student registration, club leader panels, and an encrypted Administrator portal.
2. **Event & Club Management:** Publish college events, register attendees, and manage student club memberships (with accept/reject/remove controls).
3. **Announcements & Resource Hub:** Share priority notices targeted to specific departments and upload/download lecture notes and question papers.
